Butter Chicken

A creamy and flavorful Indian dish made with tender chicken in a rich tomato-based sauce.

Number of Servings: 5

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2.5 lb store brand frozen boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 cup store brand nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tbsp garam masala (or curry powder)
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Cooking spray

Sauce

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1.5 cups frozen diced onion
  • 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp garam masala (or curry powder)
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (skip if using curry powder)
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 cups frozen peas

Rice

  • 1.25 cups long grain white rice (dry)
  • 2.5 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Tools

  • Large skillet (12 inch) or wide pot
  • Medium pot with lid
  • Cutting board and knife
  • Mixing bowl
  • Strainer
  • Measuring cups and spoons

Instructions

Rice

  1. Add rice, 2.5 cups water, and salt to the medium pot.
  2. Bring to a boil on high heat.
  3. Cover and turn heat to low.
  4. Cook 18 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  5. Turn off heat. Let it sit covered 5 minutes.
  6. Fluff with a fork. Done when the water is gone and the rice is soft.

Chicken

  1. Heat the skillet on medium high for 2 minutes.
  2. Spray the pan with cooking spray.
  3. Add half the chicken in one layer.
  4. Cook 5 to 6 minutes, flipping once. Done when the outside is browned. It can still be pink inside.
  5. Move the chicken to a plate.
  6. Repeat with the rest of the chicken.

Sauce

  1. Turn heat to medium.
  2. Add butter to the same skillet.
  3. Add frozen onion on top of the melted butter.
  4. Cook 5 minutes, stirring. Done when the onion is soft and see-through.
  5. Add garlic, ginger, tomato paste, and all sauce spices on top of the onion.
  6. Stir 1 minute until it smells strong.
  7. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, water, salt, and sugar.
  8. Stir and bring to a bubble.
  9. Add the browned chicken and its plate juices back into the sauce.
  10. Cover. Turn heat to medium low.
  11. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring once.
  12. Add frozen peas into the sauce. Simmer 3 more minutes.
  13. Check chicken: no pink inside, or 165°F on the thermometer.
  14. Turn off heat. Wait 2 minutes.
  15. Stir in the 1/2 cup Greek yogurt. Adding it off heat keeps it from splitting.
