Chorizo Breakfast Burritos

High protein chorizo and egg breakfast wraps for meal prep! I got this recipe from a video by Chef Jack Ovens.

Number of Servings: 5

Ingredients

  • 4.5 oz store-brand Mexican pork chorizo (half a 9 oz tube)
  • 8 oz store-brand 93/7 ground turkey
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup carton liquid egg whites
  • 10 oz frozen chopped spinach (1 box)
  • 3/4 cup store-brand shredded reduced-fat cheddar
  • 5 burrito-size (10 inch) flour tortillas, store brand
  • Cooking spray
  • Salt and pepper
  • Salsa or hot sauce, for serving (optional)

Tools

  • 12 inch nonstick skillet
  • Large bowl and medium bowl
  • Whisk or fork
  • Spatula
  • Clean dish towel
  • Foil or plastic wrap
  • Gallon freezer bag

Instructions

  1. Microwave the frozen spinach in a bowl for 3 to 4 minutes to thaw.
  2. Let the spinach cool for 5 minutes.
  3. Dump the spinach into the dish towel.
  4. Twist and squeeze out all the water over the sink.
  5. Slit the chorizo casing with a knife.
  6. Squeeze out half the chorizo. Wrap and freeze the rest.
  7. Crack the 6 eggs into the medium bowl.
  8. Pour in the 1 cup of egg whites.
  9. Add a pinch of salt and pepper.
  10. Whisk until smooth.
  11. Heat the skillet on medium for 1 minute. With a small pan, cook the meat in 2 batches so it browns instead of steams.
  12. Add the chorizo. Break it up with the spatula.
  13. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until it gets dark red and the fat melts out.
  14. Add the turkey to the same skillet, on top of the chorizo.
  15. Break up the turkey and mix it into the chorizo.
  16. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring often.
  17. It's done when no pink shows.
  18. Scoop the meat into the large bowl. Leave the fat in the pan.
  19. Add the squeezed spinach to the same skillet, into the leftover fat.
  20. Stir and cook for 2 minutes until hot.
  21. Add the spinach to the large bowl, on top of the meat.
  22. Spoon out most of the fat left in the pan. Leave a thin coat.
  23. Set the heat to medium-low.
  24. Pour in the egg mix.
  25. Let it sit for 30 seconds.
  26. Gently fold the eggs from the edges to the middle with the spatula.
  27. Keep folding for 2 to 3 minutes.
  28. Take the pan off the heat while the eggs still look soft and a little wet. They finish cooking from the leftover heat.
  29. Add the eggs to the large bowl, on top of the spinach.
  30. Gently mix everything together.
  31. Let the filling cool for 10 minutes. Hot filling steams the tortillas and makes them soggy.
  32. Wrap 1 tortilla in a damp paper towel and microwave for 15 seconds so it bends without cracking.
  33. Lay the tortilla flat.
  34. Scoop 1/5 of the filling (about 1 cup) into the lower middle.
  35. Sprinkle about 2 Tbsp of cheese on top.
  36. Fold in both sides.
  37. Fold the bottom edge up over the filling.
  38. Roll it up tight.
  39. Wipe out the skillet and set it on medium heat. No oil.
  40. Put the wrap in seam-side down.
  41. Toast for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  42. Flip and toast for 1 to 2 minutes more.
  43. Set it on a plate to cool all the way.
  44. Wrap it tight in foil or plastic wrap.
